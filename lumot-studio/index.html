About Us

👨‍💻

Manuel Ryan S. Habijan

BSIT 2-1 • 19 years old • FEU Roosevelt, Marikina

I am the owner and developer of Lumot Studio. I started growing moss on my study desk and I loved how calm a tiny jar can make a room. This website is my class project and the first online home of my terrarium business.

I want to share this hobby with other students and families who want a low-maintenance plant for small spaces.
